# ShapeShifter
School project. Grit Games class. Group A.

Platformer with a shape shifting character, shifting between a sphere, a cube and a pyramid, for different platforming capabilities.

The sphere is practical for moving around fast and freely. Can roll down hills for gaining momentum, and use ramps to jump far. Is tricky to control when there are narrow bridges, as the rolling nature of it makes it hard to stop from rolling over edges.

The cube is slower for moving as it rolls like a cube does. However, the cube sticks to surfaces that are angled in a way that the sphere cannot. Perhaps it is even sticky and can hold on to flat walls. It is optimal for moving over narrow bridges as it never moves without the players input.

The pyramid is upside down, never rotates, but is constantly bouncing on its sharp edge. Making it optimal for crushing things. The constant bouncing is not optimal for detailed platforming however. Possibly there are platforms that is sensitive and absolutely must NOT be jabbed by sharp edges...
